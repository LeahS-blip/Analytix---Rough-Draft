Getting your numbers in

You do this once. There are three ways, depending on what you have.

  1. Connect a source. Link Vercel, Google Analytics, Cloudflare or Plausible. KeepStat takes a snapshot every day after that, without you doing anything. If you can, start here.
  2. Upload a CSV or JSON. Drag in an export you already have. KeepStat works out which column is the date and which is visitors, then backfills your whole timeline.
  3. Paste a screenshot. No export button? Paste a picture of the dashboard. AI reads the numbers off it, you check them, and only then are they saved. The image is kept next to the data as a receipt.

It works with Vercel, Google Analytics, Cloudflare, Plausible, Umami, Stripe, and any CSV or JSON file. Shopify is coming soon.

After that, it just keeps going

Every day KeepStat saves a dated snapshot of your numbers. If your provider wipes the past later, your copy doesn't change. What you get out of it:

  • 6-month, 1-year and 3-year views, and this month next to the same month last year.
  • Any week you remember, with the exact numbers.
  • A CSV or a report you can share, whenever you want. Your data leaves as easily as it came in.
