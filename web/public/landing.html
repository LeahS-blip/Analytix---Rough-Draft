DayTodayyou connect KeepStat, once

Get your numbers in once. Keep them for good.

  1. 01
    Connect a source.

    Link Vercel, Google Analytics, Cloudflare or Plausible. From then on KeepStat takes a snapshot every day by itself. Start here if you can.

  2. 02
    Upload a CSV or JSON.

    Drag in an export you already have. KeepStat works out which column is the date and which is visitors, then backfills your whole timeline.

  3. 03
    Paste a screenshot.

    No export button? Paste a picture of the dashboard. AI reads the numbers, you check them, then they're saved. The image stays next to the data as a receipt.
