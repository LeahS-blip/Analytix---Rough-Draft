The 30-day cliff

Right now your history dies twice.

First your provider forgets it. Then your backup — a Google Sheet you dread opening — falls apart. Analytix exists because both of those already happened to us.

Problem 01 · The provider

Vercel remembers a month. That's it.

On the free plan you get a rolling 30-day window. Every day, the oldest day silently drops off the edge. Launch metrics, your first viral spike, last quarter — gone, with no export and no way back.

So it all gets smooshed into a spreadsheet.
